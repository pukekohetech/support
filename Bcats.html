Consent to assess: PHS currently holds Building, Construction, and Allied Trades Skills consent through Level 3, so the selected 405xx standards sit inside the school’s existing domain/level consent. A separate programme approval/accreditation question only arises if the school intends to award the full New Zealand Certificate.
2027 transition: the old BCATS unit standards replaced by these skill standards remain expiring with a last assessment date of 31 December 2027. For a new 2027 course, use the 405xx resources and A/M/E guidance below rather than building new material around the outgoing standards.
