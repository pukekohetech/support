Help & shortcuts
  1. Select the course first. Year 9–10 plans use 3 lessons per week; Year 11–13 plans use 4 periods per week.
  2. Add units with NZQA lookup where relevant. Credit estimates start at 5 teaching hours per credit, but the teaching duration remains editable in whole lessons / periods.
  3. Click a block in the Planning Bank, then hover and click the destination week. The tooltip shows the hidden lesson / period position without drawing subdivisions across the week.
  4. Use Assessment follows unit when the assessment should move automatically with the unit. Moving the assessment manually unlinks it.
  5. For Reduced teaching dates, enter the estimated lessons / periods lost in each affected week. Blackout weeks can be excluded completely.
  6. Each term header shows placed teaching load against available capacity. Orange/red indicates that the term is close to or beyond capacity.
  7. Drag the grip at the right edge of a block, or use its arrow keys, to adjust duration one lesson / period at a time.
  8. Use Undo after moves, resizes, deletions, date changes, auto-arrange or clear-placement actions. Ctrl/Cmd+Z also works when you are not typing in a field.
  9. Choose Year overview or Two-term / semester before printing. Use Save calendar PNG when you want the calendar as an image. Saved plans autosave locally; Export backup before moving computers.
