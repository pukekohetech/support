What the spacing means

The spacing is measured from the centre of one saw cut to the centre of the next. Closer cuts allow a tighter bend.

What the radius means

The inside radius is measured from the centre of the curve to the inside face of the plywood.

Calculation used
Kerf geometry is theoretical. Actual bending depends on plywood construction, veneer direction, defects, moisture, cut accuracy and springback.
